# rewards/

**Planned, not yet designed.**

Future home of a rewards contract for:

- Referral payouts (signed word-of-mouth introductions, per the
  "Yellow Pages of Agents" vision)
- Good-behavior badges/incentives for humans and agents
- Creator milestones funded from the treasury's 2% fee stream

## Constraints (Brandon's rules)

- $0 operating cost — rewards must be funded from earned fees, never from
  a pre-minted token or platform subsidy.
- No escrow — the contract must not custody user funds.
- Hedera only.

No Solidity here yet. Design starts after the core refactor lands and the
wallet flow is proven on real devices.
